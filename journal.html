Start here

The Career Journal is a season-by-season notebook for your Football Manager career. It sits alongside Recruitment Lab but works on its own: it doesn’t need an export, and nothing you write leaves this browser.

  1. Start a journal. Type your club, your manager’s name and the season you’re starting in, written yyyy/yy as it appears in your save. It works the same whether it’s your first season or your tenth.
  2. Add your photo and badge. Open Settings (or tap your photo or the badge at the top of the journal) to upload a manager photo and the club badge.
  3. Write the objective and build your tactic. Section 02 is the season’s objective; section 03 is where you create your tactics.
  4. Keep writing through the season. Each section follows the calendar: the summer window, pre-season, August to January, the January window, the run-in, the review and the summer after.
  5. Back up now and then. Settings → Download backup. The journal reminds you after two weeks.

Journals and seasons

  • One journal per career save. Switch journals, or start a new one, from the club box at the top of the sidebar. Each journal has its own seasons, tactics, badge and currency.
  • Shared across journals: your manager name, photo and Club Philosophy & Goals, because those usually travel with you when you change clubs.
  • Seasons sit in the bar above the sections. Start the next season when the summer arrives: last season’s projected depth chart becomes the new season’s depth chart, and the season’s tactic carries over. Earlier seasons stay as you left them.
  • Adding or deleting a season: Settings → Seasons. Type a season to add an earlier one, or use × to delete a season and everything in it.
  • Saving is automatic. The sidebar shows Saving and then Saved.

The sections

  • 01 Club Philosophy & Goals: your identity as a manager. Shared by every journal.
  • 02 Season Objective: the targets for the season. It shows again in the Season Review so you can judge the season against it.
  • 03 Tactic Ideas: the tactics you build, with roles and notes.
  • 04 Youth Setup, 05 Summer Transfer Planning, 12 January Transfer Planning: free text, with prompts for the things worth covering for each player.
  • 06 Transfers Ledger: every completed deal and the season’s net spend.
  • 07 Depth Chart and 16 Projected Depth Chart: three deep in every position of your tactic, now and for next season.
  • 08 Pre-Season, 09 August – January, 13 February – Season End: notes on form, fitness, attitude and who’s earning their place.
  • 10 Tactical Assessment: what the system is actually doing before you change anything.
  • 11 Tactical Adjustments: a text box to type the changes you’ve made, when, why and what happened.
  • 14 Season Review and 15 Summer Scouting & Transfer Targets close the season and start the next one.
  • The dots in the index on the left fill in as sections get content. Expand all opens every section; the journal remembers which ones you keep open.

Tactic Ideas

  • Create a tactic for each shape you use. Name it, pick one of 16 formations and choose the role and duty for every position from FM24’s role list. The small pitch shows the shape as you build it.
  • Each tactic has notes for in possession, out of possession, set pieces and anything else.
  • Use this season marks the tactic you’re playing this season. The depth charts follow it, and it carries into the next season when you start it.
  • Tactics belong to the journal, so every season can use them. Duplicate a tactic to try a variation without losing the original.

Depth charts

  • Every position in the tactic gets three rows: first choice, second and third. Each row has a role (leave it blank to use the tactic’s role) and a player.
  • Names you’ve typed before are suggested as you type.
  • Each depth chart can follow the season’s tactic or a different one, from the menu at the top.
  • The projected depth chart is next season’s plan. When you start the next season it becomes that season’s depth chart.

Transfers Ledger

  • One row per deal: player, in or out, the other club and the fee. Fees can be written like 12M, 500K or 2.5m; free transfers and loans count as zero.
  • Spent, received and net spend add up underneath. Choose € £ or $ in Settings.

Season Review

  • Your Season Objective is shown at the top. Mark it achieved, partly achieved or missed.
  • Record the league position, points, cup runs, top scorer, most assists, player and young player of the season, the signing of the season and the biggest disappointment, then tell the story of the season in your own words.

Screenshots

  • Add screenshots at the bottom of any section, or paste one (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V) while you’re working in a section.
  • Tap a screenshot to see it full size, give it a caption, change its type or move it to another section.
  • Gallery shows every screenshot of the season, filtered by type.
  • Large images are shrunk when you add them so the journal doesn’t fill your browser’s storage.

Export a season

  • Export season builds one page with everything you wrote that season: objective, tactics with their pitches and notes, the ledger, depth charts, review, notes and, if you like, screenshots.
  • Download the page saves it as a web page you can open on any device or share. Print or save as PDF opens your browser’s print window; choose Save as PDF there.

Backups, phones and installing

  • Where it lives: in this browser on this device. It stays when you close the page, but clearing the browser’s site data, or using a private window, loses it. The sidebar says Saved on this device.
  • Backups: Settings → Download backup saves the whole journal, images included, as one file. A reminder appears above the sections if it’s been more than two weeks.
  • Phone and laptop: download a backup on one, move the file to the other (AirDrop, email, a cloud drive or a message to yourself) and use Load a backup. If that journal is already there, you can replace it or keep both.
  • Install as an app: open the journal from the hosted link. In Chrome or Edge use Install app in the sidebar; on Android use the browser menu → Install app; on iPhone or iPad open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installed, it opens full screen and works without a connection.
  • The journal and Recruitment Lab keep separate storage, so each has its own backups.

Troubleshooting

  • “Not being saved” in the sidebar: the browser is blocking storage, usually because it’s a private window. Open the journal in a normal window.
  • Everything disappeared: the browser’s site data was cleared, or you’re in a different browser or a private window. Load your latest backup.
  • The depth chart says to create a tactic first: it lists the positions of a tactic, so build one in Tactic Ideas.
  • Nothing happens when I paste a screenshot: click into a section first, then paste. Some browsers only paste images copied from a screenshot tool, not image files.
  • Opened from a file on an iPhone: Safari may not keep notes for pages opened from the Files app. Use the hosted link instead.
