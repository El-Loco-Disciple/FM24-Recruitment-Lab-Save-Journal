RECRUITMENT LAB TUTORIAL
========================

This tutorial takes you from a fresh download to a working recruitment
department: exporting from Football Manager, setting up your club, finding a
signing for your weakest position, and keeping track of players across a season.
It takes about 30 minutes the first time, and about two minutes each time after
that.

1. WHAT YOU NEED
----------------

  - Football Manager 2024 (FM24) with a save in progress. The Lab is built and
    tested with FM24 and uses its role list. Other versions are untested: FM26
    reworked roles and tactics, so its exports and roles may not match.
  - A browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer. A phone works for
    browsing and comparing once you've loaded a phone file (section 15).
  - The Lab: the hosted link, or index.html from the download.
  - The view file: views/My_Moneyball_View.fmf from the download. It's
    essentially the free FM Data Lab view with one or two extra columns, and it
    contains every column the Lab reads.

2. ADD THE VIEW TO FOOTBALL MANAGER
-----------------------------------

A view tells FM which columns to show in a list. The Lab needs a specific set of
columns, and the view sets them up in one go.

  1. Close FM, or at least leave the Player Search screen.
  2. Open FM's user folder:
     - Windows: Documents\Sports Interactive\Football Manager 20XX
     - Mac: ~/Documents/Sports Interactive/Football Manager 20XX
  3. Open the views folder inside it. If there isn't one, create it.
  4. Copy My_Moneyball_View.fmf into views.
  5. In your save, go to Scouting → Players → Player Search.
  6. Click the view menu at the top of the list (the icon that lets you pick a
     view), choose Custom → Import View and pick the file.

The view now appears under your custom views, and FM remembers it in every save.

    | Using your own view? It must include at least Name, Position, Club,
    | Division, Age and Mins. Anything else missing switches off the stats that
    | need it, and the Leagues page tells you what's missing.

3. EXPORT YOUR FIRST SEASON
---------------------------

The Lab works from one snapshot of your FM world: every player in Player Search,
with their season stats.

  1. In Player Search, select the view you imported.
  2. Clear every filter (the search should cover all the leagues your save has
     loaded). Make sure your own players are included: the Lab finds your squad
     in the same export.
  3. Click in the list and select every row: Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac).
  4. Press Ctrl+P / Cmd+P to print the screen.
  5. Choose Web Page, give the file a name such as 2025-26-may.html, and save it
     somewhere easy to find.

When to export: the end of each season (around May) gives the cleanest numbers.
A January export is useful for the winter window, and section 14 shows how the
two fit together.

    | FM's stats are season-to-date. An export in October has a quarter of a
    | season's minutes, so the Lab asks how far through the season you are and
    | scales its thresholds to match.

4. IMPORT IT INTO THE LAB
-------------------------

  1. Open the Lab. The first time, it shows Welcome to Recruitment Lab.
  2. Click Import your first season (or Import season in the sidebar later on).
  3. Save: leave it on New save and give it a name, for example the club you
     manage.
  4. Season: type the season as it appears in your save, written yyyy/yy (for
     example 2031/32 if you're eight seasons in). Nothing is filled in for you,
     so it works however far into your save you are. Use the same style every
     time; seasons are ordered by this label.
  5. FM player-search export: pick the .html file.
  6. Season progress: the Lab reads the minutes in the file and suggests Full
     season, Three-quarters, Half season or Quarter season. Change it if it's
     wrong.
  7. Click Import.

A season of around 9,000 players takes a few seconds. Larger exports take
longer.

5. RUN THE SETUP
----------------

After the first import the Lab asks three things.

  1. Your league and club. Pick your league, then your club. This is how the Lab
     finds your squad, and it sets what stronger and weaker leagues mean.
  2. Starting tactic. Create my own (name it, pick one of 16 formations and
     choose the role for every position), or one of eleven starting points:
     4-3-3 possession, 4-3-3 counter-press, 4-2-3-1 balanced, 3-5-2 possession,
     4-4-2 direct, 4-2-3-1 gegenpress, 4-1-4-1 control, 3-4-3 wing-backs,
     4-1-2-1-2 narrow diamond, 5-3-2 counter and 3-4-2-1. You can change it all
     later in section 12.
  3. How you want to play. One of nine play styles: Possession, Counter-press,
     Direct, Balanced, Low block and counter, Wing play, Positional play,
     High-intensity press and Physical. This sets the philosophy weights for
     every role (section 12).

Click Save and open Home. If you choose Later, Home shows a Run the setup
button, and Settings has Run the setup again at any time.

6. READ YOUR HOME PAGE
----------------------

Home is your recruitment overview, built around your tactic.

  - Key numbers: your best XI's average role score against the median club in
    your league, your weakest slot, your shortlist, contracts ending within a
    year and the average age of your XI.
  - Squad gaps: every slot in your tactic, your best player there, his score and
    the gap to the league benchmark (the white tick). Red means below the median
    club, green above. Click a slot to open DoF picks for it.
  - DoF picks: ten suggestions for your weakest slot.
  - Shortlist pipeline: how many players are at each stage, and your latest
    moves.
  - Contracts: your players whose contracts end soonest, with a bar chart of
    contracts ending by year.
  - Saved searches and Prospects to watch for the weakest slot.
  - Data strip at the bottom: which season you're looking at, how much of it,
    and a reminder if you haven't backed up recently.

Click any player's name anywhere in the Lab to open his page.

7. FIND A SIGNING WITH DOF PICKS
--------------------------------

Open DoF picks from the sidebar or from a slot on Home.

  1. Role: the slot and role you're recruiting for. It defaults to your weakest
     slot.
  2. Transfer budget and max wage (in €M and €K a week). They're saved with your
     save.
  3. Read the five columns, two players each:
     - Dream: the best score in a stronger league who fits your budget and
       wouldn't refuse.
     - Upgrade: at least 10 points better than your starter, aged 22 to 29,
       costing up to half your budget.
     - Value: the most score per €M, cheap and with enough minutes to trust.
     - Squad player: rotation level, proven at your level or above.
     - Prospect: 21 or under, ranked against players his age.
  4. Each card shows his score in the role, the change on your starter, value,
     wage, contract, his philosophy score and any flags (contract expiring,
     mostly off the bench, big step down in level and so on).
  5. More filters limits the board by age, how far above your league his club
     is, confidence (minutes played) and contract length. Use it when the board
     keeps offering a 34-year-old or a player in a league five levels above you.
  6. The footnote names the best players left out and why: over budget, likely
     to refuse, not for sale.

Use ☆ Shortlist on a card to add him, Compare to line him up against others, or
Open for his page. Add all to the shortlists adds the whole board.

8. STUDY A PLAYER
-----------------

A player's page answers: is he good at what I need, and does he fit how I play?

  - Assess as: the role you're judging him for. It starts on his best role in
    your tactic. Change it and the whole page follows.
  - Role score: 0 to 100, a weighted average of the role's key stats as
    percentiles against players in his position.
  - Philosophy: how well his numbers fit your six principles, weighted for this
    role. Fits the idea is 70+, coachable 50 to 69, against the idea under 50.
  - Character test: pass, neutral or fail, from his personality.
  - Percentile profile: what the role needs, your philosophy principles, and
    anything else that stands out, each with the raw number and the percentile.
  - Summary: his strengths and weak spots in plain English, and Progress once
    you've imported more than once (section 14).
  - Role fit: his best roles with role and philosophy scores; click one to
    assess him in it. Could retrain into lists roles he could learn (section
    12).
  - Pipeline and notes: add him to a list, move him through the stages, and
    write notes. Notes go into the scouting report.
  - Indices, with the change on last season, and Similar players.

The copy icon next to his name copies it, ready to paste into FM's search.

9. SEARCH THE WHOLE POOL WITH EXPLORE
-------------------------------------

Explore is where you go beyond the suggestions.

  - Role search: pick a role. The chart plots its two key stats; the table ranks
    everyone by role score. Narrow the pool with the filters: leagues, league
    level, minimum minutes, age, value, wage, contract, height, foot, home-grown
    status, nationality and more. Add a condition for any stat (for example
    progressive passes per 90 at least 6).
  - Only these positions: each role searches its own position plus any you've
    added on the Philosophy score page. Players from an added position are
    tagged Retrain.
  - Profile search: describe a player as targets on the indices (ball security,
    progression, pressing and so on). Must-haves are hard minimums;
    nice-to-haves move players up.
  - Prospects: young players doing well in limited minutes, scored against
    players their age.
  - Risers: the biggest improvers on last season (needs two seasons).
  - Save this search keeps the filters. After each import, Home shows how many
    new players match.
  - The Raw numbers pill in the top bar can adjust attacking output for league
    strength. Use it as a stress test: a player who stays near the top with it
    on and off doesn't depend on the assumption.

10. COMPARE AND PLOT
--------------------

Compare holds up to four players, always shown as A (blue), B (orange), C
(green) and D (purple). Add them from any list, from a player page, or by typing
a name. Pick the role lens and the comparison pool, then read the percentile
lanes, pizza charts, role fit and where they sit against the pool. Export image
saves it as a picture.

Scatter plots any two stats, role scores or your own indices for a position
group. Drag a box around a cluster to select those players, then add them to
your shortlist or compare the top four. Build your own index from up to three
stats.

11. RUN YOUR SHORTLIST
----------------------

Each save has two lists: First team and Prospects.

  - Move players through Watching → Scouted → Bid → Signed, or mark them
    Rejected with a reason ("refused, wants Champions League").
  - The role score column shows each player's best role or a role you choose,
    and added at shows what he scored when you added him, with the change since.
  - Shadow squad places your first-team shortlist into your tactic. Each player
    goes to the slot where he scores best among the positions he can play; Move
    puts him anywhere you like, and the Lab remembers it for that tactic. Gold
    is your current starter. A slot where nobody on your list beats him links
    straight to DoF picks. The strip at the top adds up the cost of the top pick
    in each slot you'd upgrade.

12. TUNE YOUR TACTIC AND PHILOSOPHY
-----------------------------------

Tactic & best XI

  1. Pick a formation and a role for every slot. New tactic and Save tactic keep
     as many as you like.
  2. The pitch shows your best XI: each player used once, only in positions he's
     listed at, picked to maximise the total score.
  3. Move players: click a player on the pitch to choose who plays in that slot.
     The list shows everyone in your squad with his score for that role, where
     he plays now, and whether he'd be out of position. Pick a player from
     another slot and the two swap places. Players you place get a pin and stay
     put while the Lab fills the other slots around them. Reset to the best XI
     clears your placements. They're kept for each tactic and carry through to
     Home, DoF picks and the Shadow squad.
  4. Benchmark compares each slot with the median club in your league, or with a
     group of clubs you choose (Edit groups).

Home, DoF picks, the Shadow squad and the Club finder all use the tactic you've
chosen here.

Philosophy score

  1. The six principles each average a few stats. Add or remove stats with +
     stat and ×.
  2. Weights by role: click the dots. ●●● matters most, — isn't counted. A false
     nine might weight keep the ball heavily and ignore defend on the front
     foot.
  3. Apply a template resets every role's weights to one of the nine play
     styles. Restore the defaults goes back to the starting point.
  4. Search positions: add positions a role should also search. A defensive
     midfielder can make a good inverted wing-back; an attacking midfielder
     might become a false nine. Those players then appear tagged Retrain.
  5. Character test: choose whether players who fail are left out of DoF picks.

Your philosophy is shared by every save on the device, because how you want to
play usually stays the same when you change clubs.

13. WRITE A SCOUTING REPORT
---------------------------

On a player's page, pick the role in Assess as, add your notes, then click
Export report. The report fits one page: facts, the data verdict, percentile
profile, where he sits against the pool, role fit, a comparison with your
current player in that role, indices and your notes.

Download the report saves it as a web page. Open it in your browser and print to
PDF if you want a PDF.

14. THE NEXT WINDOW: RE-IMPORT AND TRACK PROGRESS
-------------------------------------------------

When the next window comes round, export again from FM (same view, no filters)
and import it.

  - Same season again (for example a January export, then the May one, both
    labelled 2031/32): the new import replaces the old one, so every page uses
    the latest numbers. The earlier import is kept for progress tracking.
  - A new season (2032/33): it's added alongside the old one, and Risers and the
    ▲▼ arrows compare the two.

After an import:

  - Home shows Since your last import for players on your lists who moved club,
    rose or fell by 5 or more in their role, whose contract now ends within a
    year, or whose value changed by a quarter or more. Mark as seen clears it.
  - Player pages show Progress: his score in the role you're assessing at each
    import, for example 31/32 ½ 74 · 31/32 79 · 32/33 ½ 83. The first time,
    click Show his … score across your imports to load the earlier ones.
  - The shortlist shows what each player scored when you added him, and the
    change since.
  - Saved searches on Home show how many new players match.

Settings shows how many earlier imports are kept, with Delete them if you need
the space.

15. TAKE IT TO YOUR PHONE
-------------------------

Your data lives on the device where you imported it, so a phone needs a copy.

  1. On your computer, open Settings → Send to phone. It saves a smaller file
     with your two latest seasons, shortlists, tactics and philosophy (around 5
     MB for a season of 9,000 players).
  2. Move the file to your phone: AirDrop, email it to yourself, a cloud drive,
     or a message to yourself.
  3. On your phone, open the Lab's link and choose Load a backup or phone file
     (on the welcome screen, or in Settings).
  4. Optional: add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. In Safari tap
     Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android use the browser menu → Install app
     or Add to Home screen.

After each import on your computer, send it again. The phone recognises the save
and offers to replace the old copy.

The phone is best for browsing, comparing and checking your shortlist. Import
and heavy filtering are easier on a computer.

16. BACK UP YOUR WORK
---------------------

  - Settings → Download backup saves everything in the current save, including
    earlier imports, as one .json file. Keep it somewhere safe.
  - Home shows a reminder in the data strip when it's been more than two weeks.
  - Load a backup or phone file restores it on any device. If the save is
    already there, you can replace it or keep both.

Your data is lost if you clear the browser's site data, uninstall the browser,
or use a private window. A backup is the only way back.

17. LOOKING FOR A NEW JOB: CLUB FINDER
--------------------------------------

The Club finder ranks every club by how ready its squad is for your football.

  1. Pick the tactic you'd bring.
  2. Each club gets its own best XI in that tactic, and a Foundation score: 45%
     tactical fit, 35% philosophy, 20% depth (its second-best player in each
     slot).
  3. Filter by top 5 or top 10 leagues, region, league level, the most gaps
     you'd accept (5 by default), what counts as a gap, XI age, and whether to
     leave out your own club and league.
  4. Presets: Ready now (few gaps), Project (good bones, a few signings),
     Rebuild (lots to do).
  5. Click a club to see its XI slot by slot, with gaps flagged.
  6. Preview as this club shows Home, DoF picks and the Shadow squad as if you
     managed there, with a budget estimated from its squad. Your save doesn't
     change, and Back to your club in the bar at the top takes you back.

Club finances, board expectations and whether the job is actually open aren't in
the export, so check those in FM.

18. SEVERAL SAVES
-----------------

Each save keeps its own seasons, shortlists, tactics, budget and settings, and
its own badge. Switch saves from the club box at the top of the sidebar, or
choose New save… there. Settings → Upload badge puts your club's badge in the
sidebar. Your philosophy is shared by all of them.

19. HOW THE NUMBERS WORK
------------------------

  - Percentiles compare a player with everyone in his position who played enough
    minutes (900 for a full season, scaled for part-seasons, never below 600)
    across all leagues in the export. 80 means ahead of 80% of them.
  - Role scores are weighted averages of a role's key stats as percentiles. If
    less than 60% of the weights can be measured, he gets no score rather than a
    misleading one.
  - Confidence bars: three at 2,000+ minutes, two at 1,200+, one below (scaled
    for part-seasons).
  - League levels come from FM's reputation stars for each league, compared with
    yours. A score earned two levels below yours rarely travels intact.
  - Likely to refuse means he plays in a stronger league than yours and has an
    ambitious or driven personality. It's a rough guide, not the game's logic.
  - The Guide inside the Lab has the full details: every calculated stat, data
    quirks in FM's export, all 115 roles and deep dives on 31 of them.

20. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------

  * "No Name column" on import
      The file isn't a Player Search export saved as a web page. Print the list
      again and choose Web Page.

  * Your club isn't in the setup list
      The export didn't include your league or your own players. Clear the
      filters in FM and export again.

  * A player has no role score
      Too few minutes, or too many of the role's stats are missing (common in
      leagues with limited data).

  * Scores look too high in a small league
      Use the league-level filters, and try the league adjustment pill in the
      top bar.

  * DoF picks keep suggesting players who won't come
      Keep Leave out players likely to refuse on, and use More filters to limit
      league level and age.

  * Everything has disappeared
      The browser's site data was cleared, or you're in a different browser or a
      private window. Load your latest backup.

  * Storage is full
      Delete old seasons or earlier imports in Settings, or saves you no longer
      use.

  * No Install app button
      Installing needs the hosted https link in Chrome or Edge. On iPhone use
      Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
