CAREER JOURNAL
==============

The Career Journal is a season-by-season notebook for your Football Manager
career. It comes in the Recruitment Lab download but works on its own: it
doesn't need an FM export, and nothing you write leaves your browser.

Open it with journal.html, from the link at the bottom of the Lab's sidebar or
the Guide, or straight from the hosted address (for example
https://your-username.github.io/recruitment-lab/journal.html).

1. WHAT IT'S FOR
----------------

Keeping the story of a save in one place: what you set out to do each season,
the tactics you built, the transfer windows, who played where, what you changed
when it went wrong and how the season ended. It follows the calendar of an FM
season, from the summer window to the review and the plans for next year.

2. START A JOURNAL
------------------

  1. Open journal.html and choose Start a journal.
  2. Type the club you manage and your manager's name.
  3. Type the season you're starting in, written yyyy/yy as it appears in your
     save (for example 2031/32). Nothing is filled in for you, so it works
     whether this is your first season or your tenth.
  4. Pick your currency (€, £ or $) for the transfer ledger.
  5. Optionally give the journal a name, then choose Start the journal.

Start a separate journal for each career save. Switch between them from the club
box at the top of the sidebar.

3. SEASONS
----------

  - The seasons sit in the bar above the sections. Click one to open it.
  - Start yyyy/yy begins the next season. Last season's Projected Depth Chart
    becomes the new season's Depth Chart, and the season's tactic carries over.
    Earlier seasons stay exactly as you left them.
  - To add an earlier season, or delete one, open Settings → Seasons. Deleting a
    season removes everything written in it, including its screenshots.

4. THE SECTIONS
---------------

  * 01 Club Philosophy & Goals
      Your identity as a manager. Shared by every journal.

  * 02 Season Objective
      The season's targets. Shown again in the Season Review.

  * 03 Tactic Ideas
      The tactics you build (section 5).

  * 04 Youth Setup
      Academy players and wonderkids, with prompts for role, who they replace
      and the development plan.

  * 05 Summer Transfer Planning
      Targets and departures before the summer window.

  * 06 Transfers Ledger
      Completed deals and net spend (section 7).

  * 07 Depth Chart
      Three deep in every position of your tactic (section 6).

  * 08 Pre-Season Performance Notes
      Training, friendlies, fitness, new signings settling in.

  * 09 August – January: Player Watch
      Form, injuries, attitude, names to flag for January.

  * 10 Tactical Assessment
      What the system is actually doing before you change anything.

  * 11 Tactical Adjustments
      A text box: type the changes you've made, when, why and what happened.

  * 12 January Transfer Planning
      Gaps that have shown up since August.

  * 13 February – Season End: Player Watch
      The run-in, who has earned their place, contract conversations.

  * 14 Season Review
      How the season went (section 8).

  * 15 Summer Scouting & Transfer Targets
      Targets, contracts to watch, free agents, loan recalls.

  * 16 Projected Depth Chart – Next Season
      Next season's plan, signings and academy graduates included.

Everything saves as you type. The dots in the index on the left fill in when a
section has content, and the journal remembers which sections you keep open.

5. TACTIC IDEAS
---------------

  1. Choose Create a tactic.
  2. Give it a name and pick one of 16 formations.
  3. Choose the role and duty for every position. The list is FM24's roles. A
     small pitch shows the shape as you build it.
  4. Add notes for in possession, out of possession, set pieces and anything
     else.
  5. Choose Use this season on the tactic you're playing this season. The depth
     charts follow it.

Tactics belong to the journal, so every season can use them. Duplicate copies a
tactic so you can try a variation; Delete removes it from every season.

6. DEPTH CHARTS
---------------

  - Every position of the tactic gets three rows: first choice, second and
    third.
  - Each row has a role (leave it blank to use the tactic's role) and a player.
    Names you've typed before are suggested.
  - The menu at the top lets a depth chart follow the season's tactic or a
    different one.
  - The Projected Depth Chart is next season's plan and becomes the next
    season's depth chart when you start it.

7. TRANSFERS LEDGER
-------------------

  - One row per completed deal: player, in or out, the other club and the fee.
  - Fees can be written like 12M, 500K or 2.5m. Free transfers and loans count
    as zero.
  - Spent, received and net spend add up underneath. Change the currency in
    Settings.

8. SEASON REVIEW
----------------

  - Your Season Objective is shown at the top. Mark it achieved, partly achieved
    or missed.
  - Record the league position, points, cup runs, top scorer, most assists,
    player and young player of the season, signing of the season and biggest
    disappointment.
  - The season in your words is for the story: what worked, what didn't, what
    changes next season.

9. SCREENSHOTS
--------------

  - Add screenshots at the bottom of any section, or click into a section and
    paste one (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  - Tap a screenshot to see it full size, add a caption, change its type (Data
    Hub, Tactics, Match, Player, Transfer, Training, Other) or move it to
    another section.
  - Gallery shows every screenshot of the season, filtered by type.
  - Big images are shrunk when you add them so the journal doesn't fill your
    browser's storage.

10. EXPORT A SEASON
-------------------

Export season builds one page with everything written that season: objective,
tactics with their pitches and notes, ledger, depth charts, review, notes and,
if you tick the box, screenshots.

  - Download the page saves it as a web page you can open on any device or
    share.
  - Print or save as PDF opens your browser's print window. Choose Save as PDF
    there for a PDF.

11. MANAGER PHOTO AND CLUB BADGE
--------------------------------

Open Settings, or tap your photo or the badge at the top of the journal.

  - Upload photo sets the manager photo. It's shared by every journal on the
    device, along with your manager name and Club Philosophy.
  - Upload badge sets the club badge for this journal. It also appears in the
    sidebar.
  - Remove takes either one away.

12. BACKUPS, PHONES AND LAPTOPS
-------------------------------

Your journal lives in the browser on the device where you write it. Clearing the
browser's site data, or writing in a private window, loses it.

  - Settings → Download backup saves the whole journal (every season, tactics,
    screenshots, badge, photo and Club Philosophy) as one .json file.
  - A reminder appears above the sections if you haven't backed up for two
    weeks.
  - To move between your laptop and your phone: download a backup on one, send
    the file to the other (AirDrop, email, a cloud drive or a message to
    yourself), open the journal there and choose Load a backup. If the journal
    is already on that device, you can replace it or keep both.

The journal and Recruitment Lab keep separate storage, so each has its own
backup.

13. INSTALL IT AS AN APP
------------------------

Open the journal from the hosted link (GitHub Pages), then:

  - Chrome or Edge on a computer: Install app in the sidebar, or the install
    icon in the address bar.
  - Android: browser menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
  - iPhone or iPad: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Installed, it opens full screen and works without a connection.

14. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------

  * The sidebar says "Not being saved"
      The browser is blocking storage, usually in a private window. Open the
      journal in a normal window.

  * Everything has disappeared
      The browser's site data was cleared, or you're in a different browser or a
      private window. Load your latest backup.

  * The depth chart asks for a tactic
      It lists a tactic's positions. Create one in Tactic Ideas.

  * Pasting a screenshot does nothing
      Click into a section first, then paste.

  * Notes vanish on an iPhone
      Safari may not keep notes for a page opened from the Files app. Use the
      hosted link instead.
